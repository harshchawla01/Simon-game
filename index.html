Rules
  1. Memorize the pattern of lights and sounds carefully
  2. After the sequence plays, repeat the pattern by clicking the same buttons in the correct order
  3. Each round adds one more step to the pattern
  4. If you make a mistake, the game ends and your score is recorded
  5. Try to beat your high score and compete for a spot among top 10 on the leaderboard!
